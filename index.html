Hello! I’m a Ph.D. student at the Robotics Innovatory, Sungkyunkwan University, under the supervision of Prof. Hyouk Ryeol Choi.

Alongside my Ph.D. studies, I work with AIDIN ROBOTICS on various projects in legged robotics.

My main research interests include legged robot systems, path and motion planning, and perceptive locomotion.
